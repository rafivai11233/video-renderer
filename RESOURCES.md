# Free Resources (100% free-tier stack)

## Stock video / images
- Pixabay — https://pixabay.com (API: https://pixabay.com/api/docs/) — 100 req/hr
- Pexels — https://www.pexels.com/api/ — 200 req/hr, good fallback
- Coverr — https://coverr.co (no API, manual)
- Mixkit — https://mixkit.co (no API, manual)

## Music (royalty-free)
- `/music` folder in this repo: calm-1 / dark-1 / energetic-1 (synthesized ambient beds)
- Pixabay Music — https://pixabay.com/music/ (manual download, drop into /music)
- Free Music Archive — https://freemusicarchive.org (check license, CC tracks)
- Uppbeat — https://uppbeat.io free tier (attribution)
